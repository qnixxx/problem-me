THE THINKING DISCIPLINE_

Six moves to repeat.

01Observe

Describe the deviation without explaining it away.

02Scope

Establish where, when and how often it occurs—and where it does not.

03Hypothesize

Map plausible causes without voting them into facts.

04Test

Find evidence that could support or contradict the explanation.

05Control

Reduce harm now; plan a durable correction separately.

06Verify

Define the outcome and observation period before declaring success.

OUR STANDARD_

A persuasive story is not proof. Every example distinguishes observed facts, working hypotheses, proposed actions and outcomes that still need verification.

NEW / EVIDENCE LAB_
