Three locations. Two viable choices. One leaky roof.

01 / THE CHOICE

First decide what cannot fail

Capybara wants a reading nook. The candidates are Cedar Cabin, Willow Pavilion and Pond Loft. The MUSTs are a dry roof, safe level access and availability before dusk.

Pond Loft fails its dry-roof requirement, even though the view is excellent. Cedar Cabin and Willow Pavilion pass all MUSTs. Only those two proceed to preference scoring.

MUST: a non-negotiable condition. WANT: a preference with a weight.

02 / WEIGH THE WANTS

Make the preferences explicit

Quiet matters most (weight 5), followed by view (weight 3) and walking distance (weight 2). Each viable option receives an illustrative score from one to five, with five best. Multiply score by weight and add the results.

Cedar Cabin scores 25 + 12 + 6 = 43. Willow Pavilion scores 15 + 15 + 8 = 38. The scores are judgments that should be discussed, not objective measurements.

Invented ratings, 1–5. Weighted points = weight × score. Pond Loft fails a MUST and is excluded.

PROVISIONAL CHOICE: Cedar Cabin; Willow Pavilion remains a fallback.

03 / A WEEK LATER

A new problem needs a different analysis

Following rain, books under one Cedar window are damp; the other shelves remain dry. The problem did not appear during dry weather. A window seal was replaced the previous week.

Kepner–Tregoe Problem Analysis asks what IS affected, what IS NOT, WHEN it began and WHAT changed. The replacement seal is a hypothesis, not proof. In this fictional test, water traces lead to the seal and a controlled drip check reproduces the leak.

IS: one shelf after rain. IS NOT: the other shelves. CHANGE: a new seal.

04 / REPAIR AND VERIFY

Keep choosing and diagnosing separate

The club repairs the seal, tests it and leaves vulnerable books off the shelf until a further rain check holds. Willow remains a fallback for the interim.

Decision Analysis asked which location best met the requirements. Problem Analysis asked what caused a new deviation. Neither job is interchangeable with the other.

TAKEAWAY: choose deliberately; diagnose with distinctions; test the fix.
