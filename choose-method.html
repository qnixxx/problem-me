Choose the method.Not the favorite.

Start with the question your evidence can answer. Techniques organize thinking, but they do not turn assumptions into facts or create data you do not have.

WHEN TO LEAVE THIS TOOLSET_

For a safety-critical event, medical/aviation process, destructive engineering test, high-uncertainty statistical inference or regulated incident, use the applicable specialist methodology, risk controls and qualified practitioners. The four tools shown here are introductions—not a universal root-cause solution.

INTELLIGENT HANDOFFS_

A method rarely works alone.

Pareto → Fishbone

Counts reveal that 61% of delays relate to picking errors. Use Fishbone to map plausible reasons the picking process produces errors.

PRIORITIZE → MAP

Fishbone → 5 Whys

Evidence elevates an unverified calibration branch. Test the branch, then trace the supported control failure with a causal chain.

MAP → EXPLAIN

Problem Analysis → Decision Analysis

Once the deviation is understood well enough, compare mitigation alternatives against non-negotiable constraints, benefit and risk.

DIAGNOSE → CHOOSE

QUALITY GATES_

Five questions before you present a conclusion.

  1. Observable: Is the problem statement a deviation rather than an accusation?
  2. Comparative: What is unaffected, or what happened before the change?
  3. Traceable: Can another reviewer find the original logs, records, measurements and dates?
  4. Falsifiable: What would you expect to see if your favored cause were wrong?
  5. Actionable: Can the selected control be tested and the result monitored against a baseline?
