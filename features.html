THE PROBLEM.ME APPROACH_

Evidence before certainty.

Good problem management isn't about finding someone to blame or filling a template. It's a repeatable discipline of making uncertainty visible, testing explanations and checking whether improvements endure.

  • Be preciseDescribe the deviation and the conditions under which it occurs.
  • Stay curiousCompare competing hypotheses and deliberately seek disconfirming evidence.
  • Verify resultsSeparate containment from lasting control, with meaningful baselines and observation windows.
