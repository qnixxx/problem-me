PROBLEM ANALYSIS_

A week later, Quiet Marsh turns cloudy. 🌫️💧

Now the task is no longer to choose. It is to explain a deviation: what is happening, where, when, and how much — then compare that with where the problem is not.

WHAT_

IS: Water turns cloudy after noon.
IS NOT: Water is clear in the morning.

WHERE_

IS: North inlet side.
IS NOT: South reed bed.

WHEN_

IS: Since the new feeder was installed.
IS NOT: Before installation.

EXTENT_

IS: 1–2 hours each afternoon.
IS NOT: All day or across the whole pond.

CHANGES / DISTINCTIONS_

  • New automatic feeder installed near north inlet.
  • Only the north side receives concentrated snack pellets.
  • Afternoon feeding coincides with cloudiness.

POSSIBLE CAUSES_

  • Heavy rain runoff
  • Filter failure
  • Bird activity
  • Feeder creating excess organic load

04 // TEST THE SUSPECTS_

A possible cause must explain the whole pattern — including why the problem appears here and now, but not elsewhere or earlier.

REAL WORLD TRANSLATION_

Ponds→Vendors / projects / options
MUSTs→Non-negotiable requirements
WANT weights→Relative importance
Adverse consequences→Decision risk

REMEMBER_

Do not use the scores to manufacture certainty. The value is in forcing the team to state criteria, weights and risks explicitly. If the inputs are weak or political, a precise-looking total is still weak.

This case now demonstrates both Decision Analysis and Problem Analysis. Kepner–Tregoe also includes Situation Appraisal and Potential Problem Analysis.
