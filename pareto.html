CASE #003 // THE CHAOTIC CAPYBARA CAFÉ

ParetoRank the pain.

When everything seems important, count the recurring problems and rank them by impact. Pareto Analysis helps you focus on the few categories creating most of the trouble.

PRIORITY SIGNAL_

Three categories create four-fifths of the incidents.

The 80/20 rule is a useful heuristic, not a law. Here the data happens to land exactly at 80% after the first three categories.

→

STATUS: FIXING THE VITAL FEW
ACTION_

Start where the bars are tallest.

Capybara fixes the watermelon handling process first, then coffee temperature, then spoon replenishment. The squeaky chair can remain dramatically squeaky for another day.

REAL WORLD TRANSLATION_

Café incidents→Defects / tickets / failures
Categories→Recurring cause types
Bars→Frequency or impact
Cumulative line→Share of total impact

REMEMBER_

Pareto prioritizes; it does not explain causality. A tall bar tells you where the impact is concentrated, not why that category occurs. After choosing a priority, use a causal technique such as 5 Whys or Fishbone to investigate it.
